Tasmania 2 metre Digital Elevation Model

The Tasmania 2 metre Digital Elevation Model (DEM) is a regular grid of ground level elevation values spaced at 2 metre intervals across the extent of the land area of Tasmania and most of the associated islands, but excluding Macquarie Island.

Approximately 65% of the DEM is derived from LiDAR data, with the remainder of the surface interpolated from photogrammetric contours, spot heights and mean high water level data. Outside of LiDAR areas, the data resolution is 10 m. The projection is GDA 1994 MGA Zone 55, with vertical heights relative to AHD.

Original metadata can be found on the Tasmanian Government website.
